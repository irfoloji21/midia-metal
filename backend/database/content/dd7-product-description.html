LONGWELL · LWDD7-5-EC

A compact blower with EC speed control

The DD7-5 combines a centrifugal fan with a 200 W electronically commutated motor. Its direct-drive arrangement, rectangular outlet flange and mounting base provide a defined starting point for integrating the fan into a ventilation assembly.

The published duty points cover 852–1,400 m³/h. Select a point that meets both your airflow and pressure requirement, then check the drawing against the space and connection available.

  • Power supply: 220 V, 50 Hz
  • Control input: 0–10 V or PWM signal
  • Exact model: LWDD7-5-EC

Motor & control

Match the control to your system

LONGWELL specifies an EC motor with a 0–10 V or PWM control signal. These are control inputs; they should not be confused with the fan’s mains supply.

The catalogue lists Class F insulation, NMB ball bearings and an IP55 protection rating. Rotation is clockwise when viewed from the rotor.

Before connecting a controller, obtain the wiring information for this exact model and confirm signal compatibility. The published product page does not identify the terminal connections or a supplied speed controller.

Product questions

Frequently asked questions

Will it deliver 1,400 m³/h through any duct system?

No. That figure is the published point at zero static pressure. Ductwork, fittings and other components contribute to system resistance. Select the fan using the required airflow and pressure together.

Is DD7-5 the same as every fan sold as DD7 or DD7/7?

No. Use the complete model code LWDD7-5-EC when comparing specifications or asking for a replacement. Similar series names do not establish interchangeability.

Is a speed controller included?

The manufacturer specifies 0–10 V / PWM control capability but does not list the package contents. Confirm the controller, connectors and any other accessories required with your order.

What should I confirm before purchasing?

Confirm the duty point, electrical compatibility and mechanical fit, along with current availability, delivery arrangements, supplied accessories and applicable warranty terms. The technical data below does not establish those commercial details.
