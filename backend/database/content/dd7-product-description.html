Understanding the airflow requirement

The published performance information describes a range of operating points rather than a single airflow figure for every installation. The highest listed airflow is 1,400 m³/h at zero static pressure. At a listed static pressure of 160 Pa, the corresponding airflow is 1,134 m³/h. These values should be considered together: the airflow required by the application and the pressure at that airflow form the duty point used to assess the fan.

When preparing an enquiry, include the target airflow, the calculated system pressure and a description of the equipment or duct connection. This gives a more useful starting point than selecting by maximum airflow alone. The performance table further down this description keeps the published airflow, pressure, speed and noise readings together, making it easier to compare a proposed duty with the available data.

Motor, supply and control

This model is listed with a 200 W EC motor, a 220 V / 50 Hz supply and 0–10 V or PWM speed control. The supply rating and control signal describe different parts of the connection. A controller therefore needs to be checked against the exact motor and its connection information. The product photographs show the motor position and external cable, while the model-specific wiring information is needed to identify individual terminals and connections.

For a replacement fan, record the control arrangement already used by the equipment as well as the mains supply. Include any controller reference with your enquiry so that the intended combination can be reviewed. Match the control to your system

LONGWELL specifies an EC motor with a 0–10 V or PWM control signal. These are control inputs; they should not be confused with the fan’s mains supply.

The catalogue lists Class F insulation, NMB ball bearings and an IP55 protection rating. Rotation is clockwise when viewed from the rotor.

Product questions

Frequently asked questions

Will it deliver 1,400 m³/h through any duct system?

No. That figure is the published point at zero static pressure. Ductwork, fittings and other components contribute to system resistance. Select the fan using the required airflow and pressure together.

Is DD7-5 the same as every fan sold as DD7 or DD7/7?

No. Use the complete model code LWDD7-5-EC when comparing specifications or asking for a replacement. Similar series names do not establish interchangeability.

Is a speed controller included?

The manufacturer specifies 0–10 V / PWM control capability but does not list the package contents. Confirm the controller, connectors and any other accessories required with your order.

What should I confirm before purchasing?

Confirm the duty point, electrical compatibility and mechanical fit, along with current availability, delivery arrangements, supplied accessories and applicable warranty terms. The technical data below does not establish those commercial details.
